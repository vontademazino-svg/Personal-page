About me關於我

Maputo · Sofala · Hualien

I work where organizations lose information: in WhatsApp threads, scattered spreadsheets, and knowledge that leaves when a person leaves. 我專注於組織最容易遺失資訊的地方：散落在 WhatsApp 對話、各自為政的試算表，以及隨人員離開而流失的經驗。

At the Tzu Chi Foundation in Mozambique I work in organizational development and operations, with a focus on information management, documentation and strategic planning across our hubs in Maputo and Sofala Province and our headquarters in Taiwan.

Alongside that, I study Business Management with coursework in economics and accounting, and I build small, deployable web systems that solve a real problem for the people using them.

我在慈濟基金會莫三比克分會從事組織發展與營運工作，重點在資訊管理、文件制度與策略規劃，連結馬布多、索法拉省據點與台灣本會。

同時，我就讀企業管理學系，修習經濟學與會計學，並開發能實際部署、解決使用者真實問題的小型網站系統。
